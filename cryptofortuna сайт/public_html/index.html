Questions

Frequently Asked

Which network and coin do I send?

USDT on the BSC (BEP-20) network only. Sending on any other network (TRC-20, ERC-20, etc.) will not be detected and cannot be recovered by the bot.

Why hasn't the bot confirmed my payment?

The bot assigns you a unique exact amount when you press Participate — sending that exact figure lets it auto-detect your transfer, usually within ~30 seconds. If you sent a different amount or it still hasn't confirmed after a few minutes, paste your transaction hash (TXID) directly in the chat for manual verification.

How is the winner actually chosen?

By taking the hash of a specific future BSC block, announced in advance, and computing hash % participant count. Nobody — including the club — can predict or influence a block hash before it's mined, and anyone can verify the calculation afterward on BscScan.

What if the round takes a while to fill?

Nothing is lost — your ticket and contribution stay reserved until the round fills and the draw runs. You can check live progress any time with the 💰 Pool option in the bot.

Can I get more than one ticket?

One paid entry per person per round. You can earn extra free tickets by inviting friends — see 🔗 Invite in the bot.
